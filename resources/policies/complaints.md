# ENGLISH

### 1. Purpose

This policy establishes a fair, accessible and accountable process for receiving and resolving complaints and queries.

Kopafasta welcomes:

* Complaints;
* Questions;
* Suggestions;
* Technical-issue reports;
* Loan enquiries;
* General feedback; and
* Compliments.

Submitting a complaint is free and must not result in retaliation or unfair treatment.

### 2. Scope

This policy covers complaints about:

* Product information or advertising;
* Applications and decisions;
* Fees, interest or account balances;
* Payments and repayment allocation;
* Customer service;
* Employees, agents or partners;
* Privacy and personal information;
* Technical access;
* Fraud or unauthorized activity;
* Debt collection;
* Accessibility; and
* Any other Kopafasta service.

### 3. Submission channels

Complaints and queries may be submitted through:

* **Telephone:** [Complaints phone];
* **Email:** [Complaints email];
* **Online feedback form:** [Feedback URL];
* **Physical office:** [Office address]; or
* **Other published support channel:** [Channel].

A representative may submit a complaint for another person where authority can be reasonably verified.

### 4. Information to provide

A complainant should provide, where available:

* Full name;
* Phone number or email;
* Membership, application or transaction reference;
* Date and description of the matter;
* Amount involved, if applicable;
* Relevant supporting evidence;
* Previous attempts to resolve the matter; and
* Preferred resolution.

A person must never provide a PIN, password or one-time verification code.

### 5. Registration and acknowledgement

Kopafasta will:

1. Register the complaint;
2. Record the date and channel received;
3. Assign a unique reference number;
4. Classify its subject and priority;
5. Identify the responsible team;
6. Acknowledge receipt; and
7. Explain the expected next step.

Duplicate submissions concerning the same matter should be linked rather than counted as unrelated complaints.

### 6. Priority matters

Urgent attention should be given to reports involving:

* Suspected fraud;
* Unauthorized transactions;
* Threats or abusive recovery conduct;
* Exposure of personal information;
* A vulnerable person at immediate risk;
* Significant service interruption; or
* A deadline that may cause material loss.

Prioritization does not determine the final outcome.

### 7. Investigation

The assigned officer will review relevant records, communications, agreements, transactions and supporting evidence.

The officer may contact the complainant for clarification. The investigation must be objective and proportionate.

An employee who is the subject of a complaint, or has a material conflict of interest, should not make the final determination.

### 8. Resolution period

Kopafasta will respond and resolve complaints within the applicable period prescribed by the Bank of Tanzania and other applicable requirements.

Where a matter cannot be resolved within the applicable period, Kopafasta will, where legally permitted:

* Notify the complainant before expiry;
* Explain the reason for the delay;
* Describe the action already taken;
* State what remains outstanding; and
* Provide an expected completion date.

The system should calculate deadlines from configurable regulatory settings rather than hard-coded website copy.

### 9. Resolution response

The final response should include:

* The complaint reference;
* A summary of the issue;
* The information considered;
* The decision;
* Reasons for the decision;
* Any remedy or corrective action;
* The implementation date; and
* Available escalation options.

### 10. Remedies

Depending on the circumstances, remedies may include:

* Explanation or clarification;
* Correction of information;
* Correction of an account or payment allocation;
* Completion or reversal of an eligible action;
* Refund or compensation where due;
* Removal of an improperly applied charge;
* Apology;
* Employee or partner corrective action; or
* Improvement of a process or system.

A complaint does not guarantee the remedy requested.

### 11. Internal escalation

A dissatisfied complainant may request an internal review through the published complaints channel.

The review should be conducted by an appropriately authorized person who was not responsible for the original disputed decision where practicable.

### 12. External escalation

Where the applicable requirements are satisfied, a complainant may refer a matter to the Bank of Tanzania.

**Email:** [complaints@bot.go.tz](mailto:complaints@bot.go.tz)
**Website:** [https://www.bot.go.tz](https://www.bot.go.tz)

The complainant should retain the Kopafasta reference number, final response and supporting documents.

Any applicable external deadlines are governed by current law and regulatory requirements.

### 13. Confidentiality and data protection

Complaint records will only be accessed by authorized persons and used for investigation, resolution, oversight, reporting and lawful regulatory purposes.

Information may be shared where necessary with:

* The complainant or authorized representative;
* Relevant employees or service providers;
* Insurers or payment providers;
* Regulators;
* Law-enforcement authorities; or
* Other parties authorized by law.

### 14. Records

Kopafasta will maintain records of:

* Complaints received;
* Categories and products involved;
* Acknowledgement and resolution dates;
* Decisions and remedies;
* Outstanding matters;
* Escalations;
* Recurring causes; and
* Corrective actions.

Records must be retained for the applicable legal period.

### 15. Monitoring

Management will periodically review complaint information to identify:

* Repeated product defects;
* Misleading information;
* Payment or calculation errors;
* Partner misconduct;
* Delayed resolutions;
* Accessibility problems;
* Recovery-conduct concerns; and
* Opportunities to improve products and processes.

# KISWAHILI

### 1. Madhumuni

Sera hii inaweka utaratibu wa haki, unaofikika na unaowajibika wa kupokea na kutatua malalamiko na maswali.

Kopafasta inapokea malalamiko, maswali, mapendekezo, taarifa za matatizo ya kiufundi, maswali ya mikopo, maoni ya jumla na pongezi.

Kuwasilisha malalamiko ni bure na hakutasababisha kisasi au kutendewa isivyo haki.

### 2. Wigo

Sera hii inahusu malalamiko kuhusu bidhaa, maombi, maamuzi, ada, riba, salio, malipo, huduma, wafanyakazi, mawakala, washirika, faragha, mifumo, udanganyifu na urejeshaji wa madeni.

### 3. Njia za kuwasilisha

* **Simu:** [Settings Hub];
* **Barua pepe:** [Settings Hub];
* **Fomu ya maoni:** [Settings Hub];
* **Ofisi:** [Settings Hub]; au
* **Njia nyingine iliyochapishwa:** [Settings Hub].

Mwakilishi anaweza kuwasilisha malalamiko kwa niaba ya mtu mwingine baada ya mamlaka yake kuthibitishwa.

### 4. Taarifa zinazohitajika

Mlalamikaji anapaswa kutoa jina, mawasiliano, namba ya kumbukumbu, tarehe, maelezo, kiasi kinachohusika, ushahidi na suluhisho analotaka.

PIN, nenosiri au msimbo wa uthibitishaji havipaswi kutolewa.

### 5. Usajili

Kopafasta itasajili suala, kutoa namba ya kumbukumbu, kulipanga kwa aina na umuhimu, kumpangia afisa na kuthibitisha kupokea.

Malalamiko yanayojirudia kuhusu suala moja yataunganishwa ili kuzuia rekodi mbili zisizo sahihi.

### 6. Masuala ya dharura

Kipaumbele kitatolewa kwa udanganyifu, miamala isiyoidhinishwa, vitisho, matumizi mabaya ya taarifa binafsi, mtu aliye katika hatari au hitilafu kubwa ya huduma.

### 7. Uchunguzi

Afisa atapitia rekodi, mawasiliano, mikataba, miamala na ushahidi. Anaweza kuomba ufafanuzi zaidi.

Afisa mwenye mgongano wa maslahi hatapaswa kutoa uamuzi wa mwisho.

### 8. Muda wa utatuzi

Malalamiko yatashughulikiwa ndani ya muda unaotakiwa na Benki Kuu ya Tanzania na sheria nyingine zinazotumika.

Ikiwa kuchelewa kunaruhusiwa, mlalamikaji ataelezwa sababu, hatua iliyofikiwa na tarehe inayotarajiwa.

### 9. Majibu ya mwisho

Majibu yataonyesha kumbukumbu ya malalamiko, muhtasari, ushahidi uliozingatiwa, uamuzi, sababu, suluhisho na njia ya kukata rufaa.

### 10. Suluhisho

Suluhisho linaweza kuwa ufafanuzi, kurekebisha taarifa, kusahihisha salio, kurejesha fedha zinazostahili, kuondoa gharama isiyofaa, kuomba radhi au kuboresha huduma.

### 11. Rufaa

Mlalamikaji asiporidhika anaweza kuomba mapitio ya ndani. Inapofaa, anaweza kuwasilisha suala Benki Kuu ya Tanzania:

**Barua pepe:** [complaints@bot.go.tz](mailto:complaints@bot.go.tz)
**Tovuti:** [https://www.bot.go.tz](https://www.bot.go.tz)

### 12. Usiri na kumbukumbu

Taarifa zitafikiwa na watu walioidhinishwa pekee. Kopafasta itahifadhi kumbukumbu za malalamiko, muda wa utatuzi, maamuzi, suluhisho, rufaa na hatua za marekebisho.

### 13. Ufuatiliaji

Kopafasta itatumia taarifa za malalamiko kutambua makosa yanayojirudia, taarifa za kupotosha, makosa ya malipo, mwenendo wa washirika na maeneo ya kuboresha huduma.
