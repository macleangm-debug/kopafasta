# ENGLISH

### 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Kopafasta Microfinance Limited approaches lending responsibly, transparently and fairly.

Its objectives are to:

* Help applicants make informed borrowing decisions;
* Reduce the risk of unaffordable or unsuitable lending;
* Ensure that loan costs and obligations are explained clearly;
* Protect applicants, borrowers and guarantors from unfair conduct;
* Support members experiencing genuine financial difficulty; and
* Establish clear channels for questions and complaints.

This public policy does not replace a loan agreement. The signed loan agreement and applicable product terms govern each approved facility.

### 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

* Public loan information and advertising;
* Loan enquiries and applications;
* Applicant screening and affordability assessment;
* Credit assessment and approval;
* Loan offers and contracting;
* Guarantor and security arrangements;
* Disbursement and repayment;
* Arrears management and debt recovery;
* Refinancing or restructuring where offered;
* Complaints relating to lending; and
* Employees, agents, affiliates and service partners participating in these activities.

### 3. Responsible-lending principles

Kopafasta will seek to ensure that lending decisions are based on:

1. **Affordability:** The applicant should have a reasonable ability to meet repayments.
2. **Suitability:** The product and amount should be appropriate for the applicant’s disclosed circumstances.
3. **Transparency:** Material costs, risks and obligations must be disclosed before acceptance.
4. **Fair treatment:** Applicants and borrowers must be treated respectfully and without unlawful discrimination.
5. **Informed consent:** A loan must not be accepted through deception, coercion or hidden terms.
6. **Privacy:** Personal information must be used and protected lawfully.
7. **Proportionate recovery:** Collection action must be lawful, respectful and proportionate.
8. **Accountability:** Lending decisions and material changes must be appropriately recorded.

### 4. Advertising and product information

Kopafasta will not knowingly publish information that is false, misleading or likely to create an incorrect expectation of guaranteed approval.

Public product information should clearly identify, where applicable:

* Eligibility requirements;
* Available loan amounts;
* Repayment periods;
* Indicative or applicable pricing;
* Required security or guarantors;
* Application or post-approval charges;
* Important exclusions;
* Whether an illustration is only an example; and
* How to obtain complete product terms.

Statements such as “instant,” “guaranteed” or “no checks” must not be used unless they are factually and legally accurate.

### 5. Application and assessment

Every application is subject to assessment. Submission of an application does not guarantee approval.

Kopafasta may consider:

* Identity and KYC status;
* Age and legal capacity;
* Employment, income or business activity;
* Existing financial commitments;
* Repayment history;
* Credit-reference information;
* Requested amount and purpose;
* Available disposable income;
* Guarantors or security;
* Fraud and financial-crime indicators;
* Product-specific requirements; and
* Other information reasonably relevant to the application.

Kopafasta may request supporting documents or clarification. If material information cannot be verified, the application may be delayed, declined or referred for further review.

### 6. Affordability

Kopafasta will use reasonable information available at the time of assessment to determine whether proposed repayments appear manageable.

Where the requested amount appears unaffordable, Kopafasta may:

* Approve a smaller amount;
* Offer a different repayment period where permitted;
* Request additional supporting information;
* Recommend that the applicant reconsider the application; or
* Decline the application.

An applicant remains responsible for assessing their own circumstances and disclosing all material commitments accurately.

### 7. Loan offer and disclosure

Before acceptance, the applicant must be given access to the applicable:

* Approved principal amount;
* Interest rate or pricing method;
* Fees, taxes and other charges;
* Repayment amount and frequency;
* Repayment dates;
* Total amount payable;
* Late-payment consequences;
* Security or guarantor requirements;
* Early-repayment terms;
* Default and recovery provisions;
* Complaint channels; and
* Other material contractual conditions.

Kopafasta will provide this information in a reasonably clear and understandable format.

Applicants should ask for clarification before accepting anything they do not understand.

### 8. Consent and contracting

A loan agreement may only be concluded after the required disclosures and acceptance steps have been completed.

Kopafasta will not knowingly:

* Add undisclosed charges;
* Change accepted terms without lawful authority and notice;
* Preselect optional paid products deceptively;
* Pressure an applicant into accepting immediately; or
* Treat an incomplete application as consent to borrow.

Electronic acceptance, PIN confirmation or another approved method may constitute acceptance where legally valid and clearly presented.

### 9. Guarantors and security

Where a guarantor or security is required, Kopafasta will explain the relevant obligations before acceptance.

A guarantor should be informed about:

* The obligation being guaranteed;
* The maximum or applicable exposure;
* Circumstances in which the guarantee may be enforced;
* The duration of the guarantee; and
* How to ask questions or raise a complaint.

Kopafasta will not knowingly misrepresent a guarantee as a formality without financial consequences.

### 10. Disbursement

Approved funds will be disbursed only after applicable conditions have been satisfied. Depending on the product, payment may be made to:

* The borrower;
* A verified supplier;
* A service provider;
* Another approved destination; or
* A payment channel selected under the agreement.

The destination may be verified before disbursement to reduce fraud and misdirection.

### 11. Repayment difficulties

Members should contact Kopafasta as soon as they anticipate difficulty making a repayment.

Depending on the circumstances, agreement and applicable rules, Kopafasta may consider:

* Explaining the outstanding balance;
* Correcting a verified payment-allocation error;
* Agreeing on an appropriate communication arrangement;
* Reviewing available restructuring or rescheduling options; or
* Referring the matter for additional support.

Such assistance is not guaranteed and does not automatically waive or amend a contractual obligation.

### 12. Arrears and debt recovery

Kopafasta and its authorized recovery partners must act lawfully and respectfully.

They must not:

* Use violence or threats;
* Harass or humiliate a borrower;
* Publish a borrower’s debt;
* Disclose debt information to unauthorized persons;
* Impersonate a court, regulator or police authority;
* Use misleading documents;
* Demand an amount not supported by the account; or
* Seize property without lawful authority.

Reasonable recovery costs may only be applied where permitted, disclosed and supported by the applicable agreement and law.

### 13. Fair treatment and vulnerability

Kopafasta will not discriminate unlawfully on the basis of gender, disability, ethnicity, religion, marital status or another protected characteristic.

Where reasonably identifiable, additional care should be taken when assisting a person who:

* Has difficulty understanding the product;
* Has a disability affecting communication;
* Is experiencing serious financial distress;
* Has recently suffered bereavement or illness;
* May be subject to coercion or financial abuse; or
* Requires information in an accessible format.

Fair treatment does not require Kopafasta to approve an application that fails its lawful lending requirements.

### 14. Declined applications

Kopafasta may decline an application where:

* Eligibility requirements are not met;
* Affordability is insufficient;
* Required information is missing or inconsistent;
* Identity cannot be verified;
* Fraud or financial-crime concerns exist;
* The requested product is unavailable;
* Risk exceeds approved limits; or
* Approval would breach a legal or regulatory requirement.

Where legally and operationally appropriate, the applicant may be given a general explanation. Kopafasta may be unable to disclose confidential fraud, AML or proprietary risk controls.

### 15. Member responsibilities

Applicants and borrowers must:

* Provide accurate and complete information;
* Disclose material financial commitments;
* Review the loan agreement;
* Protect account and PIN information;
* Use funds only for lawful purposes;
* Make repayments when due;
* Retain payment evidence;
* Notify Kopafasta of material changes; and
* Contact Kopafasta promptly when repayment difficulty arises.

False or misleading information may result in rejection, restriction, recovery action or lawful reporting.

### 16. Personal information and credit reporting

Personal information will be processed according to applicable law and Kopafasta’s Privacy Policy.

Where permitted, information may be verified or shared with:

* Credit reference bureaus;
* Identity-verification providers;
* Payment providers;
* Authorized service partners;
* Regulators;
* Law-enforcement authorities; or
* Other parties required or permitted by law.

Consent will be obtained where consent is the applicable legal basis.

Management is responsible for implementing this policy. Relevant personnel and partners must receive appropriate instructions or training.

Kopafasta will periodically review:

# KISWAHILI

### 1. Madhumuni

Sera hii inaeleza jinsi Kopafasta Microfinance Limited inavyotoa mikopo kwa uwajibikaji, uwazi na haki.

Malengo yake ni:

* Kuwasaidia waombaji kufanya maamuzi yenye uelewa;
* Kupunguza utoaji wa mikopo isiyomudika;
* Kueleza gharama na wajibu kwa uwazi;
* Kulinda waombaji, wakopaji na wadhamini;
* Kuwasaidia wanachama wenye changamoto halisi za kifedha; na
* Kutoa njia wazi za maswali na malalamiko.

Sera hii ya umma haibadilishi mkataba wa mkopo. Mkataba uliokubaliwa na masharti ya bidhaa ndiyo yanayotawala mkopo husika.

### 2. Wigo

Sera hii inahusu matangazo ya mikopo, maombi, tathmini ya uwezo wa kulipa, maamuzi ya mkopo, mikataba, wadhamini, dhamana, utoaji wa fedha, marejesho, madeni yaliyochelewa, urejeshaji wa madeni na malalamiko.

Pia inawahusu wafanyakazi, mawakala, washirika na watoa huduma wanaoshiriki katika shughuli hizo.

### 3. Kanuni za msingi

Kopafasta itazingatia:

1. **Uwezo wa kulipa:** Mwombaji awe na uwezo unaoonekana wa kumudu marejesho.
2. **Ufaafu:** Bidhaa na kiasi viendane na hali iliyowasilishwa.
3. **Uwazi:** Gharama, hatari na wajibu muhimu vielezwe kabla ya kukubali.
4. **Haki:** Waombaji na wakopaji watendewe kwa heshima bila ubaguzi haramu.
5. **Ridhaa yenye uelewa:** Mkopo usikubaliwe kwa udanganyifu, shinikizo au masharti yaliyofichwa.
6. **Faragha:** Taarifa binafsi zitumike na kulindwa kisheria.
7. **Urejeshaji unaolingana:** Hatua za kudai deni ziwe halali na za heshima.
8. **Uwajibikaji:** Maamuzi na mabadiliko muhimu yaandikwe.

### 4. Matangazo na taarifa za bidhaa

Kopafasta haitatoa taarifa za uongo, zinazopotosha au zinazotoa matarajio yasiyo sahihi ya kuidhinishwa moja kwa moja.

Taarifa za bidhaa zitaonyesha, inapohusika:

* Masharti ya kustahili;
* Kiasi cha mkopo;
* Muda wa marejesho;
* Riba, ada na gharama;
* Dhamana au mdhamini;
* Masharti muhimu;
* Kama mfano uliotolewa ni makadirio tu; na
* Jinsi ya kupata masharti kamili.

### 5. Maombi na tathmini

Kutuma ombi hakuhakikishi kuidhinishwa.

Tathmini inaweza kuzingatia utambulisho, mapato, biashara, madeni yaliyopo, historia ya marejesho, taarifa za mikopo, madhumuni ya mkopo, uwezo wa kulipa, wadhamini, dhamana na masharti ya bidhaa.

Taarifa zisipothibitishwa, ombi linaweza kucheleweshwa, kukataliwa au kupelekwa kwa uchunguzi zaidi.

### 6. Uwezo wa kulipa

Kopafasta itatumia taarifa zinazopatikana kutathmini kama marejesho yanaweza kumudika.

Kiasi kilichoombwa kisipomudika, Kopafasta inaweza:

* Kuidhinisha kiasi kidogo;
* Kupendekeza muda tofauti unaoruhusiwa;
* Kuomba ushahidi zaidi;
* Kumshauri mwombaji atafakari upya; au
* Kukataa ombi.

### 7. Ofa na taarifa za mkopo

Kabla ya kukubali, mwombaji ataoneshwa kiasi kilichoidhinishwa, riba, ada, ratiba ya marejesho, jumla ya malipo, madhara ya kuchelewa, masharti ya mdhamini au dhamana, kulipa mapema, kushindwa kulipa na njia za malalamiko.

Mwombaji anapaswa kuomba ufafanuzi kabla ya kukubali jambo asilolielewa.

### 8. Marejesho yenye changamoto

Mwanachama anayetarajia kushindwa kulipa kwa wakati anapaswa kuwasiliana na Kopafasta mapema.

Kopafasta inaweza kutathmini msaada unaoruhusiwa, lakini msaada huo hauhakikishwi na haubadilishi mkataba moja kwa moja.

### 9. Kudai na kurejesha madeni

Kopafasta na washirika wake hawataruhusiwa:

* Kutumia nguvu au vitisho;
* Kumdhalilisha au kumsumbua mkopaji;
* Kutangaza deni hadharani;
* Kutoa taarifa kwa watu wasioidhinishwa;
* Kujifanya mahakama, polisi au mdhibiti;
* Kutumia nyaraka za kupotosha; au
* Kuchukua mali bila mamlaka ya kisheria.

### 10. Wajibu wa mwanachama

Mwanachama anatakiwa kutoa taarifa sahihi, kueleza madeni muhimu, kusoma mkataba, kulinda PIN, kutumia fedha kwa shughuli halali, kulipa kwa wakati na kutoa taarifa kuhusu mabadiliko yanayoathiri marejesho.
