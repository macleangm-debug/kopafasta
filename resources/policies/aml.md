# ENGLISH

### 1. Purpose

Kopafasta is committed to preventing its services from being used for money laundering, terrorist financing, proliferation financing, fraud or other financial crime.

This public policy explains our general approach without revealing confidential detection rules, investigation methods or reporting thresholds.

### 2. Scope

The policy applies to:

* Applicants and members;
* Directors and beneficial owners;
* Guarantors and representatives;
* Employees and contractors;
* Affiliates and service partners;
* Suppliers and capital partners;
* Transactions and repayments;
* Digital channels; and
* New and existing business relationships.

### 3. Risk-based approach

Kopafasta applies controls proportionate to the assessed risk.

Relevant factors may include:

* Customer type;
* Product or service;
* Transaction pattern;
* Delivery channel;
* Geographic exposure;
* Ownership structure;
* Source and destination of funds;
* Politically exposed person status;
* Sanctions exposure;
* Adverse information; and
* Unusual or unexplained behaviour.

Higher-risk relationships may require enhanced due diligence, additional approval or closer monitoring.

### 4. Customer due diligence

Kopafasta may:

* Identify and verify an applicant;
* Verify representatives and their authority;
* Identify beneficial owners;
* Understand the purpose of the relationship;
* Establish an expected activity profile;
* Obtain source-of-funds information;
* Obtain source-of-wealth information where appropriate;
* Screen relevant parties; and
* Update information during the relationship.

### 5. Politically exposed persons

A politically exposed person relationship is not automatically prohibited.

Kopafasta may apply enhanced controls, including:

* Senior-management approval;
* Additional verification;
* Establishing source of funds or wealth;
* Reviewing close associates and family relationships where legally relevant; and
* Enhanced ongoing monitoring.

### 6. Sanctions

Kopafasta may screen persons, businesses and transactions against applicable sanctions and restricted-party information.

A service may be delayed, blocked, rejected or reported where required by law.

### 7. Transaction monitoring

Kopafasta may monitor activity to identify transactions that:

* Are inconsistent with known circumstances;
* Lack an apparent lawful purpose;
* Are unusually complex;
* Involve unexplained third parties;
* Appear structured to avoid controls;
* Rapidly move funds without clear reason;
* Use suspicious documents; or
* Present another financial-crime concern.

A flagged transaction is not automatically proof of wrongdoing.

### 8. Suspicious activity reporting

Where legal reporting conditions are met, Kopafasta may report information to the Financial Intelligence Unit or another competent authority.

Kopafasta may be prohibited from informing a person that:

* A report has been made;
* A report is being considered;
* An investigation exists; or
* An authority has requested information.

### 9. Prohibited conduct

No person may use Kopafasta to:

* Handle criminal proceeds;
* Conceal ownership or origin of funds;
* Finance terrorism or prohibited activity;
* Evade sanctions;
* Impersonate another person;
* Use forged or altered documents;
* Operate an undisclosed account for another person;
* Split transactions to avoid controls; or
* Mislead Kopafasta about a transaction.

### 10. Refusal, restriction and termination

Kopafasta may delay, refuse, restrict or terminate a relationship where:

* Required information is not provided;
* Information cannot be verified;
* Documents appear false;
* Activity is inconsistent or suspicious;
* Risk exceeds the approved risk appetite;
* An authority issues a lawful instruction; or
* Continuing the relationship would breach the law.

Kopafasta may be unable to disclose the complete reason where confidentiality restrictions apply.

### 11. Employees and partners

Relevant employees and partners must:

* Follow approved AML procedures;
* Complete required training;
* Escalate warning signs;
* Protect confidential information;
* Avoid tipping off;
* Maintain proper records; and
* Cooperate with authorized investigations.

### 12. Records

Kopafasta will retain applicable identification, verification, transaction, monitoring and reporting records for the period required by law.

Records must be accurate, secure and retrievable by authorized persons.

### 13. Data protection

AML information will be processed for lawful compliance, crime prevention and risk-management purposes.

It may be shared with regulators, the Financial Intelligence Unit, law-enforcement authorities, verification providers and other authorized recipients.

### 14. Governance

The Board or appropriate governing authority is responsible for oversight. Management must maintain appropriate controls, reporting and training.

The designated compliance function is responsible for day-to-day coordination, escalation and lawful reporting.

### 15. Review

The AML framework will be reviewed in response to:

* Legal or regulatory change;
* New products or countries;
* Emerging financial-crime risks;
* Control failures;
* Audit findings; or
* Material changes in operations.

# KISWAHILI

### 1. Madhumuni

Kopafasta imejizatiti kuzuia huduma zake kutumiwa kwa utakatishaji fedha, ufadhili wa ugaidi, ufadhili wa uenezaji wa silaha, udanganyifu au uhalifu wa kifedha.

Sera hii ya umma haitoi mbinu za siri za uchunguzi, viwango vya kuripoti au alama za hatari.

### 2. Wigo

Inawahusu waombaji, wanachama, wamiliki halisi, wadhamini, wawakilishi, wafanyakazi, washirika, wauzaji, miamala na mahusiano mapya au yaliyopo.

### 3. Mfumo unaozingatia hatari

Tathmini inaweza kuzingatia aina ya mteja, bidhaa, muamala, nchi, umiliki, chanzo cha fedha, hadhi ya mtu mashuhuri kisiasa, vikwazo na shughuli zisizo za kawaida.

Hatari kubwa inaweza kuhitaji uchunguzi wa kina, idhini ya juu au ufuatiliaji zaidi.

### 4. Uchunguzi wa mteja

Kopafasta inaweza:

* Kutambua na kuthibitisha mwombaji;
* Kuthibitisha mwakilishi na mamlaka yake;
* Kutambua wamiliki halisi;
* Kuelewa madhumuni ya uhusiano;
* Kubaini shughuli zinazotarajiwa;
* Kuomba chanzo cha fedha au utajiri;
* Kuchunguza orodha za hatari; na
* Kusasisha taarifa.

### 5. Watu mashuhuri kisiasa

Hadhi ya mtu mashuhuri kisiasa haimaanishi kukataliwa moja kwa moja. Uchunguzi wa kina na idhini ya kiwango cha juu vinaweza kuhitajika.

### 6. Ufuatiliaji wa miamala

Kopafasta inaweza kufuatilia miamala isiyoendana na hali ya mwanachama, isiyo na madhumuni ya wazi, inayohusisha watu wasioeleweka, iliyogawanywa ili kukwepa udhibiti au inayotumia nyaraka zenye mashaka.

### 7. Kuripoti shughuli zenye mashaka

Masharti ya kisheria yakitimia, taarifa zinaweza kutolewa kwa Kitengo cha Kudhibiti Fedha Haramu au mamlaka nyingine.

Sheria inaweza kuzuia Kopafasta kumjulisha mtu kuhusu taarifa au uchunguzi huo.

### 8. Matumizi yaliyokatazwa

Hairuhusiwi kutumia Kopafasta kuficha fedha za uhalifu, kufadhili ugaidi, kukwepa vikwazo, kujifanya mtu mwingine, kutumia nyaraka bandia au kugawa miamala ili kukwepa udhibiti.

### 9. Kuzuia au kusitisha huduma

Kopafasta inaweza kuchelewesha, kukataa, kuzuia au kusitisha huduma ikiwa taarifa hazijatolewa, haziwezi kuthibitishwa, zinaonekana bandia, shughuli zina mashaka au sheria inahitaji hatua hiyo.

### 10. Wafanyakazi na washirika

Watu husika lazima wafuate taratibu, wapate mafunzo, waripoti viashiria, walinde usiri na wahifadhi kumbukumbu.

### 11. Kumbukumbu na faragha

Taarifa za utambulisho, miamala na uchunguzi zitahifadhiwa kwa usalama kwa muda unaotakiwa kisheria na kutolewa kwa watu au mamlaka zilizoidhinishwa pekee.

### 12. Usimamizi

Bodi au mamlaka husika itasimamia sera. Menejimenti na kitengo cha uzingatiaji vitawajibika kwa utekelezaji, mafunzo, ufuatiliaji na utoaji wa taarifa za kisheria.
