# ENGLISH

### 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Kopafasta Microfinance Limited (“Kopafasta”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and how we send electronic messages such as SMS, email, and in-app notices.

### 2. Essential cookies

We use essential cookies so that you can:

* Stay signed in securely;
* Keep your language and country preferences;
* Protect forms against forgery; and
* Complete payments and account actions safely.

These cookies are required for the service to work. They are not used to advertise products to you.

### 3. Optional analytics

Where enabled in Settings, limited analytics cookies may help us understand how public pages perform so we can improve clarity and reliability. You can block non-essential cookies in your browser. Essential cookies may still be set.

### 4. Electronic communications

We send transactional messages that are necessary to operate your account, including:

* One-time passwords and security alerts;
* Payment prompts and payment confirmations;
* Membership, loan, and application status notices; and
* Service messages required by law or your agreement.

Marketing or promotional messages are sent only where you have opted in, or where the law allows. You can stop marketing messages using the instructions in the message or through your account settings where available.

### 5. Retention and control

Cookie lifetimes follow the purpose of each cookie. Session cookies expire when you close the browser. Preference cookies may last longer so your language and country remain set.

You can clear cookies in your browser at any time. Clearing essential cookies may sign you out or reset preferences.

### 6. Contact

Questions about cookies or electronic communications may be sent to our support contacts published in Settings Hub / the website footer.

# KISWAHILI

### 1. Madhumuni

Sera hii inaeleza jinsi Kopafasta Microfinance Limited (“Kopafasta”) inavyotumia vidakuzi na teknolojia zinazofanana kwenye tovuti yetu, na jinsi tunavyotuma ujumbe wa kielektroniki kama SMS, barua pepe, na arifa ndani ya programu.

### 2. Vidakuzi muhimu

Tunatumia vidakuzi muhimu ili:

* Uendelee kuwa umeingia kwa usalama;
* Lugha na nchi ulizochagua zihifadhiwe;
* Fomu zilindwe dhidi ya udanganyifu; na
* Malipo na hatua za akaunti zikamilike salama.

Vidakuzi hivi vinahitajika ili huduma ifanye kazi. Havitumiwi kutangaza bidhaa kwako.

### 3. Uchanganuzi wa hiari

Pale inapowezeshwa katika Mipangilio, vidakuzi vichache vya uchanganuzi vinaweza kutusaidia kuelewa jinsi kurasa za umma zinavyofanya kazi ili tuboreshe uwazi na uaminifu. Unaweza kuzuia vidakuzi visivyo muhimu kwenye kivinjari chako. Vidakuzi muhimu bado vinaweza kuwekwa.

### 4. Mawasiliano ya kielektroniki

Tunatuma ujumbe wa muamala unaohitajika kuendesha akaunti yako, ikiwa ni pamoja na:

* Nenosiri la mara moja na arifa za usalama;
* Maombi ya malipo na uthibitisho wa malipo;
* Arifa za uanachama, mkopo, na hali ya maombi; na
* Ujumbe wa huduma unaohitajika na sheria au makubaliano yako.

Ujumbe wa masoko au matangazo hutumwa tu pale umekubali, au pale sheria inaporuhusu. Unaweza kusimamisha ujumbe wa masoko kwa kutumia maelekezo kwenye ujumbe au mipangilio ya akaunti pale inapopatikana.

### 5. Uhifadhi na udhibiti

Muda wa vidakuzi unafuata madhumuni ya kila kidakuzi. Vidakuzi vya kipindi huisha unapofunga kivinjari. Vidakuzi vya mapendeleo vinaweza kudumu ili lugha na nchi yako zibaki.

Unaweza kufuta vidakuzi kwenye kivinjari chako wakati wowote. Kufuta vidakuzi muhimu kunaweza kukutoa nje au kuweka upya mapendeleo.

### 6. Mawasiliano

Maswali kuhusu vidakuzi au mawasiliano ya kielektroniki yanaweza kutumwa kwa anwani za msaada zilizochapishwa katika Kituo cha Mipangilio / sehemu ya chini ya tovuti.
