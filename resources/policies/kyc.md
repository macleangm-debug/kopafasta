# ENGLISH

### 1. Purpose

This policy explains why and how Kopafasta verifies applicants, members, businesses, beneficial owners and authorized representatives.

KYC helps Kopafasta:

* Confirm identity;
* Prevent fraud and impersonation;
* Meet legal obligations;
* Assess eligibility;
* Understand the intended relationship;
* Protect members and the platform; and
* Maintain accurate records.

Completing KYC does not guarantee approval for a product.

### 2. When verification is required

Verification may be required:

* During registration;
* Before submitting or approving an application;
* Before disbursement;
* When adding a business or representative;
* When account details change;
* When identification expires;
* When unusual activity is detected;
* During periodic review; or
* When required by law or risk assessment.

### 3. Individuals

An individual may be required to provide:

* Full legal name;
* Date and place of birth;
* Nationality;
* Residential and postal address;
* Phone number and email;
* National Identification Number;
* Passport, voter identification, driving licence or another accepted document;
* Clear images of required document sides;
* Photograph, selfie or liveness check;
* Signature;
* Occupation or employer;
* Business information;
* Income information;
* Source of funds; and
* Other reasonably required information.

The exact requirements must depend on the product, country, legal requirements and verification level.

### 4. Businesses and organisations

A business may be required to provide:

* Registered name;
* Trading name;
* Registration or incorporation number;
* Tax identification number;
* Registered and operating address;
* Nature of business;
* Business licence;
* Directors, partners or office bearers;
* Shareholding and control structure;
* Beneficial owners;
* Authorized representatives;
* Bank or payment details;
* Source of funds;
* Financial information; and
* Applicable licences or permits.

### 5. Beneficial ownership

Kopafasta may identify the natural person who ultimately owns, controls or benefits from a business or arrangement.

Applicants must not conceal ownership by using nominees, relatives, employees or other intermediaries.

Where ownership is complex, Kopafasta may request organizational charts, registers, agreements or additional declarations.

### 6. Representatives

A person acting for another individual or business must provide:

* Their own identity information;
* Evidence of authorization;
* Information about the person represented; and
* Any supporting resolution, power of attorney or mandate.

Kopafasta may confirm the authority directly with the represented person.

### 7. Document standards

Documents should be:

* Valid and unexpired;
* Genuine and unaltered;
* Clear and readable;
* Complete;
* Consistent with the application; and
* Issued by an acceptable authority.

Kopafasta may reject screenshots, cropped images, obscured documents or documents that cannot be authenticated.

### 8. Verification methods

Information may be verified using:

* Original documents;
* Government or authorized databases;
* National identification services;
* Credit reference bureaus;
* Trusted verification providers;
* Biometric or liveness checks;
* Phone or email confirmation;
* Employer or business confirmation;
* Physical verification; or
* Other lawful sources.

No single method is guaranteed to be available for every applicant.

### 9. Enhanced due diligence

Additional information may be required where risk is higher, including cases involving:

* Politically exposed persons;
* Complex ownership;
* High-risk geography;
* Unusual transactions;
* Significant adverse information;
* Unclear source of funds;
* Non-face-to-face registration;
* Third-party payments; or
* Inconsistent documents.

### 10. Ongoing verification

KYC is not necessarily a one-time exercise.

Kopafasta may request updated information when:

* A document expires;
* Personal or contact details change;
* Business ownership changes;
* A new product is requested;
* Activity differs from expectations;
* A periodic review becomes due; or
* A legal or risk requirement changes.

### 11. Failed or incomplete verification

Kopafasta may delay, restrict, reject or close access where:

* Required information is missing;
* Information is inaccurate or inconsistent;
* Identity cannot be verified;
* Documents appear forged or altered;
* A representative lacks authority;
* Ownership cannot be established; or
* Continuing would breach the law.

Kopafasta may be unable to disclose confidential fraud or AML reasons.

### 12. Member responsibilities

Applicants and members must:

* Use their true identity;
* Submit genuine documents;
* Provide accurate information;
* Keep information updated;
* Respond to reasonable verification requests;
* Protect credentials;
* Avoid allowing another person to use their account; and
* Report suspected identity misuse promptly.

### 13. Data protection

KYC information will be collected and used for lawful identity verification, eligibility assessment, service delivery, fraud prevention, risk management and compliance.

Kopafasta will use appropriate safeguards to protect the information.

Information may be shared with authorized verification providers, credit reference bureaus, regulators, law-enforcement bodies and other parties where legally permitted.

### 14. Retention

KYC records will be retained for the legally required period, including where a relationship has ended.

When retention is no longer required, information will be securely deleted, anonymized or otherwise handled according to the applicable retention policy.

A person may request correction of inaccurate personal information, subject to identity verification and applicable law.

# KISWAHILI

### 1. Madhumuni

Sera hii inaeleza kwa nini na jinsi Kopafasta inavyothibitisha waombaji, wanachama, biashara, wamiliki halisi na wawakilishi.

KYC husaidia kuthibitisha utambulisho, kuzuia udanganyifu, kutimiza sheria, kutathmini ustahiki, kulinda wanachama na kuweka kumbukumbu sahihi.

Kukamilisha KYC hakuhakikishi kuidhinishwa kwa bidhaa.

### 2. Wakati wa uthibitishaji

Uthibitishaji unaweza kuhitajika wakati wa usajili, maombi, kabla ya kutoa fedha, kuongeza biashara au mwakilishi, kubadilisha taarifa, kuisha kwa kitambulisho, mapitio ya mara kwa mara au pale sheria na hatari vinapohitaji.

### 3. Watu binafsi

Mtu anaweza kuombwa kutoa:

* Jina kamili la kisheria;
* Tarehe na mahali pa kuzaliwa;
* Uraia;
* Anwani;
* Simu na barua pepe;
* Namba ya Kitambulisho cha Taifa;
* Pasipoti au kitambulisho kingine kinachokubalika;
* Picha wazi za nyaraka;
* Picha binafsi au uthibitishaji wa uhai;
* Sahihi;
* Taarifa za kazi au biashara;
* Mapato; na
* Chanzo cha fedha.

### 4. Biashara na taasisi

Biashara inaweza kuombwa jina lililosajiliwa, jina la biashara, namba ya usajili, TIN, anwani, leseni, aina ya biashara, wakurugenzi, washirika, wamiliki halisi, wawakilishi na taarifa za kifedha.

### 5. Wamiliki halisi

Kopafasta inaweza kumtambua mtu halisi anayemiliki, kudhibiti au kunufaika na biashara.

Hairuhusiwi kuficha umiliki kupitia ndugu, wafanyakazi, majina ya watu wengine au mipangilio mingine.

### 6. Wawakilishi

Mwakilishi lazima athibitishe utambulisho wake na mamlaka ya kumwakilisha mtu au biashara. Kopafasta inaweza kuthibitisha mamlaka hiyo moja kwa moja.

### 7. Viwango vya nyaraka

Nyaraka lazima ziwe halali, hazijaisha muda, hazijabadilishwa, zinaonekana vizuri, ni kamili na zinaendana na taarifa za maombi.

Nyaraka zisizosomeka, zilizokatwa au zenye mashaka zinaweza kukataliwa.

### 8. Njia za uthibitishaji

Taarifa zinaweza kuthibitishwa kupitia nyaraka, mifumo ya serikali, huduma za utambulisho, kampuni za taarifa za mikopo, watoa huduma walioidhinishwa, uthibitishaji wa picha au uhai, mwajiri, biashara au uhakiki wa eneo.

### 9. Uchunguzi wa kina

Taarifa zaidi zinaweza kuhitajika kwa watu mashuhuri kisiasa, umiliki tata, maeneo yenye hatari, miamala isiyo ya kawaida, taarifa hasi, chanzo cha fedha kisichoeleweka au nyaraka zinazokinzana.

### 10. Uthibitishaji unaoendelea

Kopafasta inaweza kuomba taarifa mpya kitambulisho kinapoisha, taarifa zinapobadilika, umiliki wa biashara unapobadilika, huduma mpya inapoombwa au muda wa mapitio unapofika.

### 11. Uthibitishaji usipokamilika

Huduma inaweza kucheleweshwa, kuzuiwa, kukataliwa au kufungwa ikiwa taarifa hazipo, si sahihi, nyaraka zina mashaka, utambulisho hauwezi kuthibitishwa au kuendelea kutakiuka sheria.

### 12. Wajibu wa mwanachama

Mwanachama lazima atumie utambulisho wake halisi, atoe nyaraka halali, asasisha taarifa, alinde PIN na aripoti matumizi yasiyoidhinishwa.

### 13. Faragha na uhifadhi

Taarifa za KYC zitatumiwa kwa uthibitishaji, utoaji huduma, kuzuia udanganyifu, usimamizi wa hatari na uzingatiaji wa sheria.

Zitahifadhiwa kwa usalama kwa muda unaotakiwa kisheria na kutolewa kwa watu walioidhinishwa pekee.

### 14. Marekebisho na maswali

Mtu anaweza kuomba kusahihishwa kwa taarifa binafsi zisizo sahihi, kwa kuzingatia uthibitishaji wa utambulisho na sheria.
